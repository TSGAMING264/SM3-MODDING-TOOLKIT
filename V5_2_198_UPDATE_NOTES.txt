SM3 MODDING TOOLKIT v5.2.198 — TEX SWAPPER PACK RECOGNITION FIX
================================================================

This update keeps the v5.2.197 Browse + Image scrollbar fix and repairs pack
routing/diagnostics in Tex Swapper.

TEX SWAPPER — PACK ROUTING
--------------------------
- PC vs Xbox selection now checks the actual pack bytes as well as the filename.
- A real Xbox/X360 `mash` marker at 0x30 routes directly to the Xbox TEX reader.
- Xbox .BIN and .DAT pack/dump inputs are now included in the Browse Pack filter
  and use the Xbox route instead of accidentally falling into the PC parser.
- Existing XEPACK/XEAPK/XPACK/X360 filename routing remains supported.
- Normal PC hsam/APKF PCPACK/PCAPK/APKF inputs remain on the PC route.

TEX SWAPPER — FAILURE DIAGNOSTICS
---------------------------------
- PACK_DETECT.json is now written even when the PC universal probe rejects a pack.
- A non-NCH parse failure is no longer automatically labeled as "wrong game."
  It is reported as an unrecognized/unsupported SM3 pack layout instead.
- Actual NCH/Web of Shadows input still receives the existing wrong-game guard.

WHY THIS WAS NEEDED
-------------------
v5.2.197 did not modify pack recognition; its only functional change was the
Browse + Image scrollbar. The existing Tex Swapper route used a narrower Xbox
extension check than the Xbox extractor service itself. That allowed legitimate
SM3 Xbox inputs such as .BIN/.DAT files or renamed `mash`-header packs to be sent
to the PC parser and rejected.
